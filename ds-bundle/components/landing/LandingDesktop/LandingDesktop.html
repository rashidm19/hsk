Read the options before the audio starts. The 5-second gap is enough to predict the topic.
2
In dialogues, the answer usually hides in the second speaker's line. First line sets context; second decides.
3
Distrust literal matches. If an option repeats the audio word-for-word, it's a trap more often than not.
Traps · Confusable pairs The mistakes that cost real points
二VS两
Both mean "two" — but before a measure word it's always 两个人 ✓, never 二个人 ✗
才VS就
才 = later than expected · 就 = sooner than expected. The exam tests this constantly.
再VS又
再 = again (future) · 又 = again (already happened).
